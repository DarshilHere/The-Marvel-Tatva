DESCRIPTION

This animated series explores Wakanda's untold past, focusing on its elite secret agents, the Hatut Zeraze (War Dogs). In different times, these warriors go on dangerous missions outside Wakanda. They aim to get back important vibranium items and keep Wakanda's secrets safe, showing how the nation has long impacted the world while staying hidden.
